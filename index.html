Priority Categorization Metrics

Defects logged in this report are assigned a priority level based on the following structural and safety criteria established during the inspection.

Critical Priority (Immediate Action)

Criteria: Exposed structural rebar (risk of concrete cancer/spalling), major soil washout/undermining beneath load-bearing elements, or severe safety/liability hazards (e.g., complete loss of area lighting). Requires immediate isolation and urgent contractor intervention.

High Priority (Prompt Action)

Criteria: Significant structural risk or developing safety hazard. Includes cracks > 5mm in load-bearing posts, cracks > 3mm in slabs, slab separation/subsidence allowing direct water ingress to foundations, and deep asphalt potholes.

Moderate Priority (Scheduled Maintenance)

Criteria: Active deterioration that will escalate if ignored. Includes asphalt block cracking (2-3mm), surface raveling, minor soil erosion without immediate structural threat, and moderate concrete spalling.

Low Priority (Monitoring / Routine)

Criteria: Cosmetic degradation and general wear and tear. Includes hairline shrinkage cracks (< 1.5mm), peeling paint, and minor surface wear. Addressable via standard cyclical maintenance.

Informational (Record Only)

Criteria: Not a defect. Logged observations for the committee's record, such as newly installed signage or general environmental conditions. No remedial action required.
